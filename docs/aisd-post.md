## Long (Substack / YouTube description)

Sixty Austin ISD campuses were touched by the district's closure and
consolidation plan. This two-minute map animation walks through them:
the eleven schools that closed for good in May 2026, the sixteen that
took in their students, the ones that only lost a program or gained a
boundary, and the eighteen that were on an earlier list and came off.

The reason is a decade of falling enrollment, from about 83,000 students
in 2015 to just over 69,000 today, leaving roughly 25,000 empty seats, a
budget deficit, and state orders to turn around twelve low-rated campuses.
Nearly 3,800 students were reassigned. Each line on the map runs from a
closing school to where its students went.

South Austin shows the pattern most clearly: Becker was over capacity but
closed so its dual-language program could move to Sánchez, while Dawson,
a third full, sent everyone to Galindo.

The map is interactive. Click any school for its enrollment, its role in
the plan, what happens to the building, and every source:
austin.chat/AISD
