Your geofences

A geofence is a virtual boundary on a map. Austin.chat checks whether you’re inside or outside only while you have the geofence page open and allow device location access. There’s no app to install or background tracking.

Austin.chat is open source, and we don’t store your location. You can chat without signing in and choose an anonymous name or use your profile name. Chats are deleted within 24 hours after they end. You can optionally export a copy of the chat to keep.
