# Comedy Clubs, Open Mics, and Festivals in Austin, Texas (May–June 2026 Snapshot)

## Executive Summary

- Austin’s stand-up and improv scene is anchored by a tight cluster of dedicated comedy clubs downtown and in East Austin, plus a major club at The Domain and several alt‑comedy theaters.[^1][^2]
- The next 30 days (late May through late June 2026) feature nightly club shows, dense open‑mic calendars, and at least three notable festival‑style brands active in or around Austin: Moontower, SXSW Comedy, and Excused Absence / youth‑focused improv.[^3][^4][^5]
- For mapping and product‑intelligence work, the core “must‑map” layer is a set of 8+ primary venues (Comedy Mothership, Cap City, Creek & The Cave, Sunset Strip, Rozco’s, ColdTowne, Fallout Theater, East Austin Comedy, Velveeta Room), each with stable addresses and recurring shows or mics.[^2][^6][^1]

## Core Austin Comedy Venues

- **Comedy Mothership** – Joe Rogan’s flagship downtown club with two rooms (“Fat Man” and “Little Boy”), nightly ticketed shows, and heavily subscribed open mics plus the live Kill Tony podcast.[^1][^2]
- **Cap City Comedy Club** – Legacy Austin club now located at The Domain, hosting national headliners, local showcases, and contests such as “Funniest Person in Austin.”[^7][^8]
- **The Creek and The Cave** – East 7th Street club with indoor and outdoor stages, nightly programming, and a dense schedule of free open mics and development shows.[^9][^2]
- **Sunset Strip Comedy Club** – 6th Street upstairs club with stand‑up six nights a week, combining local comics and national touring acts.[^10][^11][^1]
- **Rozco’s Comedy Club** – Intimate East 7th club positioned as a “hidden gem” for stand‑up, with regular showcases and some all‑ages or brunch‑time shows.[^12][^13]
- **ColdTowne Theater** – East Austin alternative comedy theater with improv, sketch, and stand‑up seven nights a week plus classes and youth programs; host venue for Excused Absence Fest.[^6][^14][^3]
- **Fallout Theater** – Underground comedy venue on Lavaca Street with improv, sketch, and stand‑up shows nightly and a strong training‑center component.[^15][^16][^17]
- **East Austin Comedy (Club)** – Small, intimate East 6th club known for earlier‑evening shows and curated stand‑up lineups in a BYOB‑style room.[^18][^19][^20]
- **The Velveeta Room** – Long‑running Sixth Street stand‑up club next to Esther’s Follies, featuring weekly showcases and speed‑mic style nights.[^21][^22][^2]

## Festival Brands Active in Austin

- **Moontower Comedy Festival** – A Paramount‑backed festival that brings more than 100 comedians to 10+ downtown clubs every April, with a two‑week span and a 4‑day “binge” core.[^4][^28][^2]
- **SXSW Comedy** – Integrated into the broader SXSW festival every March, adding stand‑up shows, podcast tapings, and special events to the film, music, and tech programming.[^5]
- **Excused Absence Comedy Fest** – All‑ages friendly improv, sketch, and stand‑up festival centered at ColdTowne Theater; the 2026 edition is scheduled for April 11–12, 2026.[^3]
- **Out of Bounds Comedy Festival** – Multi‑day festival brand scheduled for November 5–8, 2026, featuring improv, stand‑up, and sketch across multiple Austin venues.[^29]

## Open Mic Ecosystem Overview

- The Creek and The Cave maintains one of the densest open‑mic schedules in town, including The Monday Gamble Mic, Monday Night Gimmick Mic, Dunk Tank, Off The Cuff (riff mic), Wild West Wednesdays, Bear Arms Open Mic, Word Up!, and a Sunday club open mic with email signup and bucket spots.[^9][^1]
- Comedy Mothership supports open mics through its Sunday and Monday lists and the Kill Tony live show, which uses in‑person sign‑ups at nearby Pour Choices.[^1]
- The Velveeta Room runs “Speed Mic” on Thursday nights, stacking many comics into a compact late‑night show.[^22]
- City‑wide open‑mic calendars such as Badslava’s Austin page and ComedyWham’s events calendar aggregate dozens of mics each week across bars, cafes, and clubs.[^30][^31]

## Representative Open Mics at The Creek and The Cave (Through May 31, 2026)

- **The Monday Gamble Mic** – Free weekly mic on Mondays at 6 pm; in‑person sign‑ups; 2–3 minute sets depending on format.[^9]
- **Monday Night Gimmick Mic** – Free Monday midnight mic with rotating gimmicks; comics earn an extra minute for participating in the gimmick; sign‑ups at 11:30 pm.[^9]
- **Dunk Tank** – Tuesday 6 pm show where comics try new material in a loose, workshop‑style format; sign‑up at 5:45 pm, capped at 15 comics.[^9]
- **Off The Cuff** – Wednesday 6 pm riff mic hosted by Rachel Wolfson; sign‑ups at 5:30 pm.[^9]
- **Wild West Wednesdays** – Wednesday midnight mic with a 20‑comic cap and three‑minute sets, hosted by Hunter Parker.[^9]
- **Bear Arms: Open Mic** – Thursday 6 pm mic with sign‑ups at 5:45 pm.[^9]
- **Word Up! Open Mic** – Thursday midnight stand‑up open mic.[^9]
- **The Creek and The Cave Open Mic (Sunday)** – Sunday 8 pm club mic with free entry by RSVP or small door fee, all‑day happy hour, and a combination of email pre‑booked comics plus random bucket draws.[^9]
- **Banana Phone** – Sunday 10 pm heckle‑friendly open mic where hecklers ring in; hosted by Kandace Medina.[^9]
