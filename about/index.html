Walk into a location — the chat opens

When your GPS puts you inside a geofenced spot, a chat panel opens automatically. A YOU'RE HERE badge confirms you're live.

No login, no account. Messages are ephemeral — nothing is stored. Walk out and the conversation is gone.

Reach out to other locations in the same category

Your home spot is your anchor. From it, you can open any other location in the same category and chime in — labeled from your spot so context is never lost.

Ask if another H-E-B has eggs. Warn about a long checkout line across town. The whole chain is one conversation.

Every location becomes a geofenced chat room

Each spot on the map — a campus, a store, a bar — has an invisible boundary drawn around it. Step inside and you're in the room.

The Info tab surfaces address, hours, and quick links like Waze and Reddit without leaving the map.

Over 20 categories across Austin

H-E-Bs, Walmarts, libraries, bars, food trucks, parks, campuses, transit centers, hospitals, YMCAs, golf courses — each is its own live network.

Toggle a category and only those locations light up. Everything else steps back. Switch anytime.

Built on open infrastructure — no ongoing cost

Every piece is open-source or free tier. No servers, no bills, no vendor lock-in. Fork the repo, add your spots, push to GitHub Pages — done.
